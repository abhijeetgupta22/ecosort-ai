2. Transparency & Explainability

Explainable AI (XAI)

Every inference provides an attribution summary explaining why an item was assigned to a bin and alerts users when human verification is required.

  • Resin Code Recognition: Explicitly highlights SPI 1-7 stamps to educate students.
  • Uncertainty Guardrail: Inferences with confidence < 85% prompt manual confirmation.
  • Regulatory Citations: Answers cite specific clauses in SWM Rules 2016 and E-Waste Rules 2022.

3. Ethics & Human Dignity

Worker Safety Prioritized

Prioritizes frontline sanitation workers (Safai Karamcharis) by strictly mandating paper-wrapping protocols for sharps and broken glassware.

  • Anti-Greenwashing: Rejects false recyclability claims for non-recyclable multi-layered chip packaging.
  • Fire Prevention: Prohibits lithium-ion cells in compactor trucks, preventing vehicle fires.
  • Social Inclusion: Connects segregated recyclable scrap with authorized local waste collectors.

4. Privacy & Data Governance

Zero PII Guaranteed

Strict telemetry minimization ensuring complete anonymity and zero user tracking.

  • Automatic EXIF Stripping: Removes GPS location tags and camera serial numbers before processing.
  • No Biometric / Facial Processing: Computer vision pipeline strictly isolated to inanimate waste objects.
  • Local Edge Friendly: Capable of on-device heuristic inference without third-party data transmission.
