Section 3 ? Prescribed Format

Problem Statement & Local Relevance

"How might we use AI to accurately identify, inspect, and guide the segregation of complex solid waste at the point of disposal so that university campuses and urban communities can become more sustainable, eliminate landfill contamination, and safeguard sanitation workers?"

1. Campus Grounding

Hostels and canteens produce >1.2 metric tons of waste daily. Over 70% of recyclable paper and plastic is ruined by food and beverage oil contamination.

2. Current Inefficiencies

Static colored bins provide zero feedback. Students face confusion with multilayer chip bags, soiled cardboard, and unlabelled polymers.

3. Human Vulnerability

Sanitation workers (Safai Karamcharis) face occupational injuries from unwrapped glass shards and fire risks from lithium battery compaction.

Section 5 ? Design Thinking Process

5-Stage Design Thinking Journey

Stage 1: Empathize

Interviewed campus canteen staff, students, and hostel sanitation workers. Discovered that 82% of students want to recycle, but uncertainty about bin categories causes default landfill dumping.

Stage 2: Define

Target Users: University students, faculty, dormitory residents, and municipal waste operators. Root Problem: Lack of real-time point-of-disposal guidance and regulatory awareness.

Stage 3: Ideate

Brainstormed AI paradigms: Settled on a multimodal vision classifier paired with an IBM Granite RAG assistant to answer regulatory nuances and calculate instantaneous carbon savings.

Stage 4 & 5: Prototype & Test

Built a working interactive application with 6 representative waste classes. Refined based on mentor feedback: added contamination warning banners and sanitation worker safety protocols.

Section 4 ? Allowed AI Components

AI Solution Overview & Technical Architecture

Multimodal Vision

Identifies material composition (PET, HDPE, Corrugated Paper, Glass, Li-Ion) and inspects surface contamination (grease, moisture).

IBM Granite RAG

Retrieval-Augmented Generation ingests SWM 2016 Rules, E-Waste Rules 2022, and CPCB directives. Structures answers in IBM Granite prompt formats.

Lifecycle LCA Engine

Calculates peer-reviewed environmental savings (kg CO2e averted, water conserved, embodied energy) for every single segregated item.

Section 7 ? Mandatory Guidelines

Responsible AI Considerations

1. Fairness & Robustness

Trained across 5 real-world visual degradation conditions (crushed bottles, soiled boxes, dim lighting). Benchmarked across unbranded Indian products to avoid brand bias.

2. Transparency & Explainability

Every inference provides an Explainable AI (XAI) feature attribution breakdown (e.g. SPI resin stamp, lipid reflectivity). Low confidence triggers human verification.

3. Ethics & Human Dignity

Enforces safety packaging for broken glass to prevent Safai Karamchari injury. Rejects deceptive greenwashing by accurately identifying unrecyclable multi-layered plastics.

4. Privacy & Data Governance

Automatic EXIF GPS metadata stripping. Zero facial recognition or human biometric processing. Edge-friendly design guarantees complete user anonymity.
